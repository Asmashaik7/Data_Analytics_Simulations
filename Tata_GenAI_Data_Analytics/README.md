# Tata Group – GenAI Powered Data Analytics Forage Job Simulation

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains my work for the **Tata Group – GenAI Powered Data Analytics Virtual Job Simulation** hosted on **Forage**.

In this simulation, I worked as a **Data Analyst** supporting **Geldium's Risk Assessment Team**, using **Generative AI (ChatGPT/Codey)** to solve business problems through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), predictive planning, business reporting, and AI-powered strategy design.

The simulation focused on applying AI-assisted analytics to generate business insights and communicate recommendations in a professional consulting environment.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Geldium's Risk Assessment Team wanted to leverage **Generative AI** to improve credit risk assessment and collections planning.

The objective was to analyze customer data, identify key risk factors, design a predictive modeling approach, summarize findings for stakeholders, and propose an AI-powered collections strategy that supports responsible and data-driven decision-making.
